Prompt:

Always use the latest version of uploaded files for reference and ignore previous versions.
Treat everything inside "{{ }}" as variables and replace their values with:

Role = Junior/Mid-level DevOps Engineer
CompanyName = [Company Name]
CompanyWebsite = [company website]


I'm applying for a {{ Role }} position at this company: {{ CompanyWebsite }}

Data for reference:
1. Company Name: {{ CompanyName }}
2. My research about company: `company-research.md`
3. Skils company looking for: `jd-keywords.yaml`
4. My current resume for the company: `resume.txt`  

Purpose:
I want a personalized, company-specific cover letter that demonstrates a clear understanding of the company's business, engineering challenges, and how my experience can contribute to solving them.

Write a cover letter that makes the recruiter think, &quot;This candidate understands our business, understands our engineering challenges, and has experience that is directly relevant to solving them.&quot;

Instructions:
1. Before writing the cover letter, silently identify the three strongest connections between:
  i.    the company's needs,
  ii.   the job description,
  iii.  and my experience.
  
  Build the entire cover letter around those three connections.

2. Do not summarize my resume. Explain how my experience enables me to solve the company's business and engineering challenges.
3. Connect my skills to the company's business outcomes.
4. Avoid generic enthusiasm, clichés, and buzzwords. Every sentence should communicate specific value rather than general interest.
5. Mention at least two specific facts about the company, its products, technology, engineering culture, or business direction to demonstrate genuine interest.
6. Emphasize problem-solving and measurable impact.
7. Never invent anything, that is not supported by the provided files.
8. Every important claim about my abilities must be supported by evidence from `resume.txt`. Show rather than tell.
9. Present my experience accurately. Do not exaggerate my level of responsibility, seniority, or expertise.
10. Each paragraph should have a single clear purpose. Avoid repeating the same idea across multiple paragraphs.
11. Close with confidence by expressing enthusiasm for discussing how I can contribute, rather than simply thanking the reader.
12. Target length: 250–350 words.
13. Follow this structure:

  Paragraph 1
  Why this company?

  Paragraph 2
  Why I fit this role?

  Paragraph 3
  How I can contribute?

  Closing
  Professional call to action.
