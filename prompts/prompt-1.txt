Prompt:
--------Copy below here----------

Always use the latest version of uploaded files for reference and ignore previous versions.
Treat everything inside "{{ }}" as variables and replace their values with:

Role = Junior/Mid-level DevOps Engineer
CompanyWebsite = [company website]

Purpose:
I'm applying for a {{ Role }} position at this company: {{ CompanyWebsite }}

Your objective is to help me understand the company deeply so I can:

1. Tailor my resume and cover letter
2. Prepare for interviews
3. Demonstrate genuine business understanding
4. Identify where I can realistically add value as a {{ Role }}

Role:
Act as an investigative journalist, technology analyst, and DevOps consultant.

Search the company's official website, engineering blogs, documentation, LinkedIn, Reddit, GitHub, news articles, interviews, podcasts, conference talks, employee reviews, forums, and other credible public sources.

Prefer official sources whenever possible. Use community discussions only to provide additional context and clearly distinguish facts from opinions.

Research Tasks:
  1. Company Overview:
    i.    What does the company do?
    ii.   What products or services does it offer?
    iii.  Who are its customers?
    iv.   Which industries does it serve?
  
  2. Business Model:
    i.    How does the company generate revenue?
    ii.   What makes it competitive?
    iii.  What differentiates it from competitors?
    
  3. Company Direction:
    i.    Recent announcements
    ii.   New products
    iii.  Expansion plans
    iv.   Where the company appears to be heading over the next 2–5 years

  4. Based on public information, identify their engineering challenges, and present them. Never present speculation as fact.

  6. How I Can Contribute:
    i.    Assume I join as a {{ Role }}. Suggest practical ways I could contribute using skills I have in `my-skills.yaml`
    ii.   Only recommend contributions that logically align with the company's business and engineering needs.

Interview Preparation:
  1.  Generate:
    i.    Five business-focused interview talking points
    ii.   Five DevOps-specific talking points
    iii.  Three thoughtful questions I can ask the interviewer
      
Output Requirements:
  1.  Keep the report concise, practical, and easy to skim.
  2.  Separate facts from assumptions.
  3.  Prioritize actionable insights over company history.
  4.  Avoid marketing language.
  5.  Use bullet points where appropriate.
  6.  If evidence is weak or unavailable, explicitly state that instead of guessing.
